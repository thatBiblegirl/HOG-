Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Hoops of Grace.

How do I enroll my child in the program?

Contact us via WhatsApp (click the green button below) or visit our training facility in Kampala. We conduct assessments every month to evaluate skill level and place athletes in the appropriate program tier (Foundation, Elite Development, or University Pathway).

What ages do you accept?

We accept youth ages 10-18. Our Foundation Track is for ages 10-14, Elite Development for 14-17, and University Pathway for 16-18. Athletes are placed based on both age and skill assessment.

Is there a cost to join?

Hoops of Grace is committed to accessibility. We offer sliding scale fees based on family income, with full scholarships available for those who qualify. No child is turned away due to financial constraints—basketball and education should be available to all.

What does the program include?

Complete athlete development: basketball training 3-4x per week, academic tutoring, UNEB exam preparation, mentorship, nutrition guidance, uniform and equipment, tournament participation, and university recruiting support. We develop the whole person, not just the player.

Where are training sessions held?

We train at multiple facilities across Kampala, including indoor courts and outdoor training grounds. Specific locations are shared upon enrollment. Transportation assistance is available for families who need it.

Can my child join mid-season?

Yes! We accept athletes year-round. New cohorts start quarterly, but exceptional athletes can join anytime with a coach's recommendation. Contact us to schedule an assessment and placement evaluation.
